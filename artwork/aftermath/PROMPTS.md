## Screensaver source

Source: `../../screensaver/source.png`, 1672 × 941. Generated by editing the Road of Ashes source with this prompt:

Edit target: the supplied early Edo Japanese village-road wallpaper. Create a dedicated source image for conversion to Omarchy animated ASCII art. Preserve the wide landscape composition, winding road, damaged wooden village roofs, tired packhorse and distant adult travelers, mountain ridges and low sunset. Simplify these into beautifully composed clear WHITE and light-gray silhouettes and broad contour lines on a PURE BLACK background. This is an image for conversion to a 120-column terminal, so use strong readable shapes, separated edges and large negative spaces; eliminate photographic texture and tiny debris. The tired horse on the lower left, a small walking traveler near the center, a broken roof to the left, receding mountain contours and the sun should all read as distinct recognizable forms. Restrained, solemn, early Edo atmosphere. No samurai, no extra people, no text, no logos, no borders. 16:9 composition, high contrast silhouette/contour art.

Converted to Unicode braille text art with Omarchy's native transcoder:

```bash
omarchy transcode ascii screensaver/source.png screensaver/scene.txt --width 120 --height 40 --mode braille --threshold 28 --invert --no-trim
```

The color animation is rendered at runtime by the native `ttfx colorshift` effect; it is not a video or an animated bitmap.

## Resolution exports

Both landscape sources were resized with ImageMagick's Lanczos filter to exact 2560 × 1440 and 3840 × 2160 dimensions. These are upscaled exports, not native 1440p or 4K generations. The tiny source aspect-ratio difference is absorbed by resizing to exact 16:9.

```bash
magick source.png -filter Lanczos -resize '2560x1440!' wallpaper.png
magick source.png -filter Lanczos -resize '3840x2160!' export-4k.png
```
